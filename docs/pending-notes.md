### Line: 自分などまだ吸血鬼を取り巻くコウモリ程度でしかないと
Show: Monster (2004) · animepahe · same episode as 素行調査 / 必要としていた

**For the grammar guide "noun-modifying clause" (relative clause), and the word guide 取り巻く**

How 取り巻く (とりまく) modifies コウモリ: Japanese puts the modifier before the noun, and a plain-form
verb is enough. No "that" or "which" word is needed. 吸血鬼を取り巻くコウモリ is literally
"vampires-OBJECT surround bat", i.e. "a bat that surrounds vampires". Compare 本を読む人, "a person who
reads books". The を still belongs to the verb inside the clause (吸血鬼を取り巻く = "surround
vampires"), and the whole clause hangs on コウモリ like an adjective would.

**For the word guide 程度 (and the grammar guide でしかない)**

How you know 程度 (ていど) is belittling here: on its own it is fairly neutral ("degree, level,
about"). 風邪程度 ("just a cold-level thing") or 一時間程度 ("about an hour") can sound either way.
What tips it here:

1. でしかない: "is nothing but", which makes the sentence explicitly limiting.
2. 自分など: the speaker already puts himself down.
3. The image: bats hovering around vampires are lesser followers, so "bat-level" is a low rank.
4. まだ: "still", implying he hasn't risen above that.

So 程度 contributes "a mere level", and the context makes it an insult to himself. Without しかない
(コウモリ程度だ) it still leans dismissive because of the other clues, just less strongly.

### Line: 俺が殺した連中は要らない連中なんだよ
Show: Monster (2004) · animepahe

**For the past ～た guide (built-in) and the noun-modifying clause guide**

殺した here describes 連中: 俺が殺した連中 = "the guys I killed". A plain past verb placed before a noun
works like an English relative clause, with no "that/who" word. Inside such a clause the subject takes が,
not は (俺が殺した). 要らない連中 is the same pattern with a negative: "guys nobody needs". So the line is
"The guys I killed are guys nobody needs, you know." The built-in past guide currently has no "before a
noun" use; add one.

## Guides to write

(店を出す, 歯切れが悪い, に関して and なり are now written in scripts/guides/library-fixes-ja.txt.)

- 店を出す (みせをだす): "open a shop / start a business", a set phrase. Vocab, in the "Set phrases
  and expressions" group. Met as 店を出した.
- 歯切れが悪い (はぎれがわるい): idiom, "evasive, vague, not speaking plainly" (literally "the cut of the
  teeth is bad"). Met as 歯切れが悪いようだね in the interrogation scene: "You seem rather evasive." Explain
  the image, the opposite 歯切れがいい, and that ようだね softens it into an observation with a needle in it.
- に関して (にかんして): compound particle, "regarding, concerning". Phrase guide, saved as the phrase, not
  as 関する; contrast について, に対して, に関する + noun.
- なり (grammar, rewrite): one core idea, "within X's own limits / as befits X". Lead with the line:
  君なりの正当性 = "your own (so-called) justification", the detective keeping his distance from it. なりの +
  noun vs なりに + verb/adjective; X は X なりに; attaches to nouns and to plain adjectives and verbs in this
  sense (安いなりに, 分からないなりに); look-alikes: 独自の, らしい, 自分の; separate words: Verb なり "as soon as",
  なり…なり "whether… or". No contradictions; replaces the current guide.
