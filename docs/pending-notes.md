### Line: 自分などまだ吸血鬼を取り巻くコウモリ程度でしかないと
Show: Monster (2004) · animepahe · same episode as 素行調査 / 必要としていた

**For the grammar guide "noun-modifying clause" (relative clause), and the word guide 取り巻く**

How 取り巻く (とりまく) modifies コウモリ: Japanese puts the modifier before the noun, and a plain-form
verb is enough. No "that" or "which" word is needed. 吸血鬼を取り巻くコウモリ is literally
"vampires-OBJECT surround bat", i.e. "a bat that surrounds vampires". Compare 本を読む人, "a person who
reads books". The を still belongs to the verb inside the clause (吸血鬼を取り巻く = "surround
vampires"), and the whole clause hangs on コウモリ like an adjective would.

**For the word guide 程度 (and the grammar guide でしかない)**

How you know 程度 (ていど) is belittling here: on its own it is fairly neutral ("degree, level,
about"). 風邪程度 ("just a cold-level thing") or 一時間程度 ("about an hour") can sound either way.
What tips it here:

1. でしかない: "is nothing but", which makes the sentence explicitly limiting.
2. 自分など: the speaker already puts himself down.
3. The image: bats hovering around vampires are lesser followers, so "bat-level" is a low rank.
4. まだ: "still", implying he hasn't risen above that.

So 程度 contributes "a mere level", and the context makes it an insult to himself. Without しかない
(コウモリ程度だ) it still leans dismissive because of the other clues, just less strongly.
